1. The service

Mai is a bot that:

  • Replies in character when mentioned, replied to, or sent a direct message, and may attach a GIF where a server has enabled that;
  • Reacts to certain keywords with emoji and can welcome new members;
  • Classifies messages in enabled servers for policy violations and can react to, reply to, and delete flagged messages, and send the author a warning direct message;
  • Applies, where a server switches them on, rules of its own that need no classifier: invite links, a link allowlist, a mention cap and a message-flood rule;
  • Can, where a server switches them on, place a member in a Discord timeout after repeated enforced deletions, and remove a server nickname it classifies as a violation;
  • Gives members two ways to answer back: reporting a message to staff, and appealing a warning, both of which land in that server's staff channel.

Mai is provided free of charge and is open source.

4. Moderation and enforcement

Where enabled by a server's administrators, Mai may automatically flag and delete messages it classifies as violating policy, notify the author, and - where that server has switched escalation on - place a repeat offender in a Discord timeout of increasing length. She never kicks or bans anyone on her own. Classification is automated and may produce false positives or false negatives. Moderation decisions are made by the server's administrators through their configuration of Mai; the operator of the bot is not responsible for a given server's moderation choices. If you believe a message was removed in error, use the appeal button on the warning you received (or /mai appeal if it never arrived), which puts it in front of that server's staff; a granted appeal reverses the strikes it was about.

They may be inaccurate, incomplete, or unintended, and do not constitute professional advice of any kind. Do not rely on Mai's output for any important decision. Mai's replies are posted without pinging users or roles, and @everyone/@here are neutralized.
